# Patient Pages

## 1. Patient Dashboard

### Purpose
Allow the patient to view their upcoming appointment and current queue status.

### Components
- Patient name
- Upcoming appointment
- Doctor name
- Specialty
- Appointment date
- Appointment time
- Queue number
- Appointment status
- View Queue button

### Prompt

Design a modern medical clinic patient dashboard for a smart clinic management system.

Display the patient's upcoming appointment, doctor name, specialty, appointment date and time, queue number, and appointment status.

Include a clear appointment card and a "View Queue" button.

Use a clean, professional medical design with accessible typography and a simple mobile-first layout.

### Navigation
Patient Dashboard
→ Appointment Details
→ Waiting Queue

## 3. Turn Notification

### Purpose
Notify the patient that their turn has arrived.

### Components
- Doctor name
- Queue number
- Turn status
- Confirm button
- Decline button

### Navigation

Confirm
→ Doctor Consultation

Decline
→ First Decline Warning

### Prompt

Design a medical appointment turn notification screen.

Display a prominent message:

"Your turn is now"

Show the doctor name and queue number.

Provide two actions:
- Confirm
- Decline

The Confirm action should be the primary action.

Use a clean and professional medical interface.

## 4. First Decline Warning

### Purpose
Inform the patient that they declined their turn and will be called again.

### Components
- Warning message
- Doctor name
- Queue number
- Remaining attempts
- Wait for My Turn Again button

### Navigation

First Decline
→ Waiting Queue
→ Second Turn Notification

### Prompt 

Design a warning screen for a patient who declined their turn for the first time.

Display:

"You declined your turn."

Explain that the patient will be called again shortly.

Show the remaining confirmation attempts and provide a "Wait for My Turn Again" button.

Use a professional medical design without making the warning overly alarming.

## 5. Appointment Cancelled

### Purpose

### Components

- Queue number
- Cancellation reason
- Back to Dashboard button

### Navigation

Appointment Cancelled

### Prompt 

Design an appointment cancellation screen for a smart clinic application.

Display:

"Appointment Cancelled"

Explain that the appointment was cancelled because the patient declined their turn twice.

Show:
- Doctor name
- Appointment date
- Queue number
- Cancellation reason

Include a "Back to Dashboard" button.

Use a clean and professional medical UI.
