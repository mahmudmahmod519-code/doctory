 ### Option 2: Manual Appointment Booking
    The patient can also book an appointment manually from the main page.
    1. The patient browses the available medical specialties or doctors.
    2. The patient selects the appropriate specialty or doctor.
    3. The system displays the available appointment dates and times.
    4. The patient selects a suitable appointment.
    5. The system asks for any missing personal information.
    6. The patient confirms the appointment.
    7. The system provides the patient with the appointment details, queue number, and QR Code.
